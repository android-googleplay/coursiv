### 《香港國安法》第 60 條

> 駐香港特別行政區維護國家安全公署及其人員依據本法執行職務的行為，不受香港特別行政區管轄。 持有駐香港特別行政區維護國家安全公署制發的證件或者證明文件的人員和車輛等在執行職務時不受香港特別行政區執法人員檢查、搜查和扣押。 駐香港特別行政區維護國家安全公署及其人員享有香港特別行政區法律規定的其他權利和豁免。

**課堂法律卡粗體重點**

- **不受香港特別行政區管轄**
- **不受香港特別行政區執法人員檢查、搜查和扣押**
- **享有香港特別行政區法律規定的其他權利和豁免**

**English legal text stored in the course**

> The acts of the Office for Safeguarding National Security in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and its personnel performing their duties in accordance with this Law are not subject to the jurisdiction of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. Personnel and vehicles holding certificates or certification documents issued by the Office for the Maintenance of National Security in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region are not subject to inspection, search and seizure by law enforcement officers of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region when performing their duties. The Office for the Maintenance of National Security in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and its personnel enjoy other rights and immunities provided by the laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

來源：[官方頁面](https://www.elegislation.gov.hk/hk/A302!zh-Hant-HK.assist.pdf)

### 《香港國安法》第 63 條

**中文法源文字**

> 辦理本法規定的危害國家安全犯罪案件的有關執法、司法機關及其人員或者辦理其他危害國家安全犯罪案件的香港特別行政區執法、司法機關及其人員，應當對辦案過程中知悉的國家秘密、商業秘密和個人隱私予以保密。 擔任辯護人或者訴訟代理人的律師應當保守在執業活動中知悉的國家秘密、商業秘密和個人隱私。 配合辦案的有關機構、組織和個人應當對案件有關情況予以保密。

**課堂法律卡粗體重點**

- **應當對辦案過程中知悉的國家秘密、商業秘密和個人隱私予以保密**
- **應當保守在執業活動中知悉的國家秘密、商業秘密和個人隱私**
- **應當對案件有關情況予以保密**

**English legal text stored in the course**

> Relevant law enforcement and judicial agencies and their personnel handling cases of crimes endangering national security stipulated in this Law, or law enforcement and judicial agencies and their personnel of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region handling other cases of crimes endangering national security, shall keep state secrets, business secrets and personal privacy learned during the handling of the case confidential. Lawyers who serve as defenders or litigation agents shall keep state secrets, business secrets and personal privacy that they learn during their professional activities. Relevant institutions, organizations and individuals cooperating in handling the case shall keep the relevant information of the case confidential.

來源：[官方頁面](https://www.elegislation.gov.hk/hk/A302!zh-Hant-HK.assist.pdf)

### 《香港國安法》第 64 條

**中文法源文字**

> 香港特別行政區適用本法時，本法規定的“有期徒刑”“無期徒刑”“沒收財產”和“罰金”分別指“監禁” “終身監禁”“充公犯罪所得”和“罰款”，“拘役”參照適用香港特別行政區相關法律規定的“監禁”“入勞役中心”“入教導所”，“管制”參照適用香港特別行政區相關法律規定的“社會服務令”“入感化院”，“吊銷執照或者營業許可證”指香港特別行政區相關法律規定的“取消註冊或者註冊豁免，或者取消牌照”。

**課堂法律卡粗體重點**

- **適用本法時**
- **分別指“監禁” “終身監禁”“充公犯罪所得”和“罰款”**
- **參照適用香港特別行政區相關法律規定的“監禁”“入勞役中心”“入教導所”**
- **參照適用香港特別行政區相關法律規定的“社會服務令”“入感化院”**
- **“吊銷執照或者營業許可證”指香港特別行政區相關法律規定的“取消註冊或者註冊豁免，或者取消牌照”**

**English legal text stored in the course**

> When this law is applied in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the terms "fixed-term imprisonment", "life imprisonment", "property confiscation" and "fine" prescribed in this law refer to "imprisonment", "life imprisonment", "confiscation of proceeds of crime" and "fine" respectively. "Criminal detention" refers to "imprisonment", "sentence to a labor service center" and "sentence to a training center" as stipulated in the relevant laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. "Control" refers to "social detention" as stipulated in the relevant laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. "Revocation of license or business license" refers to "cancellation of registration or registration exemption, or cancellation of license" as stipulated in the relevant laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.
